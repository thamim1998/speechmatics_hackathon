## Nutrition Guidelines (MIND Diet for Brain Health)

### Foods to Emphasize
- **Green leafy vegetables**: Spinach, salad, kale (6+ servings/week)
- **Berries**: Blueberries, strawberries (2+ servings/week)
- **Nuts**: Almonds, walnuts (5+ servings/week)
- **Whole grains**: Brown rice, whole wheat bread (3+ servings/day)
- **Fish**: Salmon, sardines (1+ serving/week)
- **Olive oil**: As primary cooking fat
- **Beans/legumes**: Lentils, chickpeas (3+ servings/week)
- **Poultry**: Chicken, turkey (2+ servings/week)
- **Vegetables**: At least 1 other vegetable per day

### Foods to Limit
- Red meat (fewer than 4x/week)
- Butter/margarine (less than 1 tbsp/day)
- Fried or fast food (less than 1x/week)
- Pastries and sweets (fewer than 5x/week)
- Excessive cheese

### Hydration
- **Target**: 8-12 cups of fluid per day
- Offer water, herbal tea, diluted juice throughout the day
- Include hydrating foods: soups, fruits (watermelon, oranges)
- Monitor for dehydration signs: dark urine, dry mouth, increased confusion
- Set regular hydration reminders every 1-2 hours

## Communication Guidelines

### How to Talk with Abhishek
- Use his name: "Abhishek" when addressing him
- Speak slowly and clearly in short, simple sentences
- Ask **one question at a time**
- Give him **at least 15-20 seconds** to respond
- Use yes/no questions when possible
- Offer simple choices: "Would you like tea or water?"
- **Never quiz or test** him -- frame everything as casual conversation
- If he repeats himself, respond patiently as if hearing it for the first time

### Validation Approach
- Enter his reality -- don't argue about facts or correct him
- Validate his emotions: "That sounds frustrating" / "I understand"
- If he is confused, gently redirect without saying "you're wrong"
- Mirror his emotional tone -- if he's happy, be happy with him
- Look for unmet needs behind any agitation (hunger, pain, boredom, fear)

### Things to AVOID
- Do NOT say: "I already told you", "Don't you remember?", "You're wrong"
- Do NOT rush him or finish his sentences
- Do NOT talk about him as if he's not there
- Do NOT use baby talk or patronizing language
- Do NOT quiz him on dates, names, or facts

## Common Challenges & Management

### Sundowning (Evening Confusion)
- Reduce stimulation after 5:00 PM
- Keep home well-lit in the evening
- Avoid caffeine after noon
- Play calming music
- If agitated: speak softly, find unmet need, offer reassurance

### Wandering Prevention
- Door alarms/sensors at apartment exit
- Familiar routes for walks with supervision
- ID bracelet with name and contact info
- GPS tracker in phone or wearable

### Sleep Disturbances
- Consistent bedtime at ~9:00 PM
- Maximize daylight exposure during the day
- Limit naps to 30 minutes max
- Dark, quiet bedroom environment
- Calming bedtime routine (music, gentle conversation)

### Agitation
- Identify root cause: pain, hunger, overstimulation, boredom
- Reduce noise and clutter
- Speak calmly and offer reassurance
- Change activity or environment
- Never confront or restrain
